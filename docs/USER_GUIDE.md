# CRISP Content Engine — User guide

This guide explains how to use the product from day one. It describes what you see on screen and what to do next—not how the product is built.

---

## What CRISP does

CRISP helps you turn your brand strategy into **channel-ready content**: drafts you can review, edit, approve, schedule, and publish. You stay in control; the product accelerates drafting and organisation.

---

## Finding your way

The main navigation groups common tasks:

- **Strategy** — View or update your content strategy.  
- **Content** — **Idea Engine**, approval queue, scheduled posts, published posts, and **Quick Generate** (individual posts).  
- Your **account menu** includes connections and billing.  

Use **Idea Engine** when you want one idea to become a full set of drafts across channels; use **Quick Generate** when you only need specific posts.

---

## Dashboard

When you sign in, the **Dashboard** is your home base.

### Recommended next step

At the top of the overview you’ll see **Recommended next step**. This suggests a single action based on where you are—for example:

- Finish setting up your brand  
- Review your strategy  
- Start your first **content series** (Idea Engine)  
- Connect social accounts for publishing  
- Review drafts waiting for approval  
- Create your next batch when you’re already up and running  
- Upgrade if you’ve used your monthly allowance  

Use it as a shortcut. You can still use the rest of the app in any order.

### Your plan and usage

Your plan and limits appear on the dashboard. **Monthly post allowances** reset on a calendar-month basis. When you’re close to your limit, you’ll see clear messaging and a path to upgrade if you need more capacity.

### Content Actions (after your strategy is approved)

Once onboarding is far enough along, you’ll see **Content Actions** on the dashboard, including:

- **How many posts you’ve used** out of your monthly total, with a simple progress view  
- **When your allowance resets** (days until the next monthly reset)  
- **Quick Generate** — create individual posts for a chosen brand within your allowance  
- **Idea Engine** — turn one idea into a coordinated set of drafts across channels (see below)  
- **Monthly strategy update** — refresh themes and objectives for upcoming content  

If you’ve reached your monthly post limit, Quick Generate is limited, but you’ll still see **Idea Engine** where your plan allows it, plus options to upgrade or wait for the next reset.

## Two ways to create content

| | **Idea Engine** | **Quick Generate** |
|---|------------------|-------------------|
| **Best for** | One idea → many related posts across channels | One-off or channel-specific generation |
| **Typical use** | Campaigns, themes, series | Single posts, fast iterations |
| **Where to start** | **Content → Idea Engine** (also on the Dashboard) | **Content → Generate** |

**Idea Engine** is the primary workflow for building a **content system** from a single idea. **Quick Generate** is there when you only need individual posts.

---

## Idea Engine (content series)

Generation runs inside CRISP when native mode is enabled (no external automation wait). You still review drafts before they enter your content queue.

### Before you start

- Choose a **brand**  
- Enter your **idea** (and optional goal or notes)  
- Pick **channels** your plan supports  
- Review the **preview** — the app shows how many posts per channel you’ll get; counts adjust automatically if you’re low on monthly allowance  

### While content is generating

You’ll see a **live workspace**: progress, staged messages, and cards filling in channel by channel. Each channel shows progress (for example, how many posts are ready out of the total). Newly finished items can briefly show a **“Just added”** indicator so you can spot fresh drafts.

If something fails, you’ll see a clear message and any **partial results** that did complete. You can **try again** or **start over** without losing context unnecessarily.

### After generation

You **review** each item: edit, remove, or regenerate as needed. When you’re ready, approved items go into your **content queue** as drafts so you can use your normal approval and scheduling flow.

---

## Content workflow: Approval → Schedule → Published

1. **Approval queue** — Drafts waiting for you to approve, edit, or reject.  
2. **Scheduled** — Content with a scheduled time (list and calendar views where available).  
3. **Published** — Content that has gone live through your connected channels or workflow.  

Empty pages use **short, contextual hints** (not generic “nothing here” text) so you know what to do next—for example, going to Idea Engine, reviewing drafts, or understanding that scheduling comes after approval.

## Brand onboarding

When you **add a brand**, you’ll walk through steps such as brand basics, audience and value, voice and rules, and platforms.  

- Progress is **saved as you go** so you can return later.  
- From step 2 onward (company brands), you can use **Skip for now** on a step if you want to move ahead and refine details later—**step 1** still captures essentials so your strategy can be generated reliably.  
- A **final step** may offer skipping optional extras if you prefer to submit with what you’ve entered.  

Completing onboarding unlocks strategy generation and content features for that brand.

## Account menu

From your profile menu you can reach:

- **Connections** — Link social accounts used for publishing  
- **Billing** — Plans, upgrades, and subscription management  

---

## Plans and fair use (high level)

- **Starter** — Core access; Idea Engine may be unavailable depending on product rules.  
- **Creator and above** — Idea Engine and higher limits unlock according to your plan.  
- **Growth and above** — Broader channel coverage (including Meta channels where applicable).  

Exact numbers and features are shown **in the app** for your account so they stay accurate when product rules change.

---

## Getting help

Use **Recommended next step** on the Dashboard when you’re unsure what to do. For billing or access issues, use **Billing** in the account menu.
